# CampusFound Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  
**Version:** 0.9.0-beta

This Privacy Policy explains what information CampusFound uses and why that information is required.

## 1. Information We Use

Depending on the information provided during registration and profile management, CampusFound may process:

- full name;
- college email address;
- phone number;
- department;
- year of study;
- account role;
- claim information; and
- information provided as part of an ownership claim.

CampusFound also stores information necessary to operate items and claims within the Lost & Found system.

## 2. Why We Use This Information

Information is used to provide CampusFound functionality, including:

- creating and managing user accounts;
- identifying authenticated users;
- displaying relevant account information;
- allowing students to submit ownership claims;
- allowing authorized administrators to review claims;
- maintaining claim history and status; and
- supporting the Lost & Found verification process.

## 3. Claim Information

Information entered when submitting an ownership claim is used to help authorized administrators evaluate whether the claimant may be the owner of an item.

Users should provide only information necessary to establish ownership.

Do not include sensitive information such as:

- passwords;
- one-time passwords (OTPs);
- banking details;
- PINs; or
- other unnecessary confidential information.

## 4. Item Information

CampusFound displays information about found items so students can identify belongings they may have lost.

Item information may include an item title, description, category, location, relevant date, image, status, and administrator-provided information.

## 5. Access to Information

Account, item, and claim information is used according to the permissions provided by CampusFound.

Students can access functionality intended for student accounts, while authorized administrators can access administrative functionality required to manage items and review claims.

## 6. Authentication and Security

CampusFound uses authenticated accounts and access controls to protect functionality that requires authorization.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials private and should not share their password or authentication information with others.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention

CampusFound retains account, item, and claim information as necessary to operate the application and support the Lost & Found process.

Retention practices may change as CampusFound develops and as operational requirements are finalized.

## 8. Third-Party Services

CampusFound may rely on third-party infrastructure or services to provide functionality such as database hosting, image storage, email delivery, application hosting, or future notification delivery.

These services may process technical information required to provide their respective functionality according to their own terms and privacy practices.

## 9. Beta Testing

CampusFound version 0.9.0-beta is currently a beta release.

During beta testing, application behavior, data handling processes, and features may be refined as the system is tested and improved.

Users are encouraged to report privacy or security concerns through the available support options.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CampusFound features or data-handling practices change.

The effective date or version should be updated whenever significant changes are made.

## 11. Questions and Concerns

Users can use the Report an Issue or Send Feedback options within CampusFound to report concerns related to the application.
