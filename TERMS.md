**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026

Welcome to CampusFound. By using CampusFound, you agree to these Terms & Conditions.

## 1. Purpose

CampusFound is a campus Lost & Found application designed to help students discover and claim belongings that have been reported to the Lost & Found system.

Items available in CampusFound are managed by authorized administrators. Students can browse available items and submit ownership claims.

## 2. User Accounts

Users are responsible for providing accurate information when creating and maintaining their CampusFound account.

You should not:

- impersonate another person;
- provide intentionally false account information;
- allow another person to misuse your account; or
- use CampusFound for purposes unrelated to its Lost & Found functionality.

## 3. Item Information

Item information displayed in CampusFound is provided and managed by authorized Lost & Found administrators.

CampusFound does not guarantee that every lost item on campus will appear in the application or that all displayed information will always be complete or error-free.

## 4. Ownership Claims

Students should submit a claim only when they genuinely believe an item belongs to them.

When submitting a claim, users may be asked to provide identifying information about the item that can help the Lost & Found team verify ownership.

Users must not intentionally submit false, misleading, fraudulent, or repeated claims for items that do not belong to them.

## 5. Claim Review

Submitting a claim does not mean that ownership has been confirmed.

Claims may be reviewed by an authorized Lost & Found administrator and may be marked as:

- Pending;
- Approved; or
- Rejected.

CampusFound does not automatically determine legal or physical ownership of an item.

## 6. Physical Verification and Collection

An approved claim does not automatically entitle the user to collect the item.

After approval, the claimant may be required to visit the official Lost & Found office and provide appropriate identification or additional information.

The item will be handed over only after the responsible administrator completes the required physical verification.

## 7. Responsible Use

Users must use CampusFound responsibly.

Misuse may include:

- intentionally submitting false claims;
- attempting to claim another person's belongings;
- providing misleading ownership information;
- attempting to interfere with the application or its services; or
- using the service for abusive, fraudulent, or unauthorized purposes.

Claims or access may be restricted when misuse is identified.
